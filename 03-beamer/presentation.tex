\section{Technical Writing}
\begin{frame}{What is Technical Writing?}
\begin{block}{Definition}
Technical writing communicates specialized information clearly, accurately and in a structured form.
\end{block}
\begin{itemize}
\item Understand the audience.
\item Organize information logically.
\item Keep formatting consistent.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}
